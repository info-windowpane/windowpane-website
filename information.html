TRANSACTION FATIGUE, Harsh Patel
14th March — 10th April 2026




Windowpane is a curatorial experiment where temporary postcard installations become studies in themes of light, display (and concealment), puzzles, and communication. The voyeuristic act of looking through a stranger's window is reciprocated with an intentional gesture of bringing something into view.

Each exhibition is made up of twenty postcards, presented as a window display and expanded as a boxed edition. Once exhibited, the display postcards are posted on to friends, family, collaborators, in order to spark new connections and projects.

Boxed editions are for sale in limited runs.
